What each stat does
PAC · Pace
Running speed, with or without the ball. A 99 is about 45% quicker than a 50. Decides who wins races and breakaways.
SHO · Shot power
How hard your shots fly. High power beats the keeper before he can dive, and powers Rockets and Knuckleballs.
FIN · Finishing
Accuracy. Low finishing sprays shots wide of where you aimed, especially at full power. Also sharpens your penalties and ultimate shots.
SKL · Skill
How often your skill moves (E) put a defender on the floor, and how close the ball stays to your feet. High skill makes you very hard to tackle.
PAS · Passing
Pass accuracy and pace. Poor passers drift off target and play softer balls that get cut out. Also makes Through Balls cleaner.
DEF · Defending
How often your lunges and slides actually win the ball when you reach it. A 90 defender wins it about twice as often as a 50.
STR · Strength
Holding off defenders. Strong players barely get bumped, can lean through weaker markers, resist tackles and knock players over when they bounce off.
