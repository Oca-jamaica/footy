What each stat does
PAC · Pace
Running speed, with or without the ball. A 99 is about 45% quicker than a 50. Decides who wins races and breakaways.
SHO · Shot power
How hard your shots fly. High power beats the keeper before he can dive, and powers Rockets and Knuckleballs.
FIN · Finishing
Accuracy. Low finishing sprays shots wide of where you aimed, especially at full power. Also sharpens your penalties and ultimate shots.
DRI · Dribbling
How often your skill moves (E) put a defender on the floor, and how close the ball stays to your feet. High skill makes you very hard to tackle.
PAS · Passing
Pass accuracy and pace. Poor passers drift off target and play softer balls that get cut out. Also makes Through Balls cleaner.
DEF · Defending
How often your lunges and slides actually win the ball when you reach it. A 90 defender wins it about twice as often as a 50.
PHY · Physical
Holding off defenders. Strong players barely get bumped, can lean through weaker markers, resist tackles and knock players over when they bounce off.

Your players

Every pack shows you three players of one rarity and you sign one. All packs are bought with coins you earn by playing: no real money, ever. Signing a player you already own keeps the duplicate for Exchanges.

Host a match

Pick the match, then press Host. You get a 4-letter code; your friend types it on their laptop.

Join a match

Got a code from a friend? Type it here.

You each play with your own team and line-up (your Volta line-up for Volta, your Classical line-up for Classical), and you both see yourself attacking left to right. After Kick off both laptops load the match and confirm they’re ready before a 3-2-1 countdown. No penalties: a draw is a draw. Coins: 60 for a win, 30 for a draw, 15 for a loss (Classical 1.5×).

Move your save to another copy of the game

Each copy of the game file keeps its own save. To carry your club across: open the copy that has your save, press Copy save code, then open the new copy, paste the code here and press Load save.

Who’s who: the real players behind every card

Every card is backed by a real footballer, and their country shows on the card. With real names on, cards use the real player’s name (any name you set yourself still wins). Search any card, fix a name or country, and press Save.

Rename players in one go

Lists the players you own. Type a name in any box, then save. Leave a box empty to keep the card’s own name. Your names are saved to your account, and your online opponent sees them too.
